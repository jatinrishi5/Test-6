02

The Practice

An independent firm, and the commitments behind it.

An independent practice, built on plain answers.

Too many people only hear from their accountant once a year, and leave without understanding what was filed. JRishiCPAs was started to do it differently: plain answers, planning before deadlines instead of after, and a reply from the person who knows your file.

The practice works entirely online, which is how it serves individuals and businesses in every province. Meetings happen by video or phone, documents move through a secure portal, and returns are filed electronically.

“A reply from me, not a call centre.”

Whether it is a first Canadian return, a side business that has grown, or a company's year-end, the approach is the same: understand the situation, explain the options in plain language, and agree the fee before any work begins.

  1. i

    A quote before we start

    The fee is agreed up front. No hourly meter, no surprise invoices.

  2. ii

    You approve before we file

    Every return is walked through with you, and every question answered, first.

  3. iii

    Direct access

    You email or call the person who prepared your file, not a support queue.

  4. iv

    Proactive, all year

    Reminders before instalments and deadlines, not just an April rush.
